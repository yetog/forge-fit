**Purpose:** A short briefing to share with a golf instructor **after the player reviews it**. This public template is deliberately generic. Keep completed personal coaching notes, videos, measurements and identities in private storage rather than this repository.

## Lesson sequence — proposed, instructor can adjust

| # | Lesson | Questions to resolve | Desired documented outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Comprehensive Swing Analysis | What differentiates clean and missed strikes? Are setup/strike/path findings repeatable? | Baseline, observed fault priority, one coach-approved cue/drill and objective retest |
| 2 | Approach and Short Game | How consistent are partial wedges, chips and landing spots? | Repeatable short-game shot options and a distance-control test |
| 3 | Iron Play | How do strike, start line, curvature and dispersion vary by iron? | Stock iron shot and comparison criteria |
| 4 | Driver, Woods, Hybrid | What is reliable from tee versus turf? How does club fitting affect the result? | Go-to long-game option and measurable carry/dispersion baseline |

## First lesson: collect evidence before changing swing mechanics

1. Agree on sample clubs and fixed conditions (target, lie, club, stance and camera).
2. Record 5–10 baseline shots per selected club (or a sample the instructor considers practical). Include good and bad outcomes.
3. For each, note clean/fat/thin/top, heel/center/toe if genuinely observed, initial direction and curvature.
4. Capture face-on and down-the-line video if possible; do not claim clubface/path readings from video alone.
5. Link launch-monitor metrics to specific shots only when shot IDs or timestamps are available. Otherwise label session summary.
6. Have the instructor separate **observed issue** from **possible cause**, then propose one controlled experiment.
7. Retest in comparable conditions. Record raw counts, measurement provenance and instructor's confidence.

## Questions for the instructor

- Which problems are confirmed by contact evidence versus inferred from ball flight?
- Does address distance vary, or does the delivery change after setup?
- What differentiates a flushed strike from a chunk under the same club and lie?
- Which single issue should the player prioritize now, and which distractions should be ignored?
- What would improvement look like objectively at the next meeting?
- Is any equipment/club-fit variable creating avoidable difficulty?

## Information handling

- Never publish completed identifying lesson notes or video in a public GitHub repository.
- Explicitly label historical recollections as unverified until original records are checked.
- Store source videos and reports only in a coach/player-approved private folder with access restrictions.
- Instructor feedback does not automatically become universally applicable app instruction. Educational reuse requires review and permission.
- This template does **not** approve any app integration or a merge.
