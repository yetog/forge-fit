# Justin — Start Here: Coaching Knowledge Handoff

**Audience:** Instructor and player. **Status:** Working brief, not instructor-confirmed diagnosis. Last organized: 2026-11-26.

This is the entry point for the coaching packet. It is designed to make **four remaining lessons** effective and to minimize time spent reconstructing the golfer's history during a paid lesson.

## 1. The 60-second overview

- Training became serious in **April 2026**. Earlier play was less structured.
- Current priority: reproducible contact, consistent address and a clear way to diagnose misses rather than changing several mechanics at once.
- Longer-range aim: play full rounds confidently and eventually enter a beginner-friendly amateur golf event.
- Player has experimented with swing cues and swing-plane visual models; please evaluate what is useful instead of accepting a self-diagnosis.
- Proposed lesson order: **(1) full swing assessment → (2) approach/chipping → (3) irons → (4) driver, woods and hybrid**. Instructor may reorder according to findings.

## 2. Observed versus hypothesized

| What the player reported | What remains open for the instructor |
|---|---|
| Some shots feel flushed, others are chunked even within a short sample | Verify impact/turf order and identify mechanically repeatable differences |
| Recent fitting conversation emphasized heel-biased strikes | Verify center/heel/toe pattern with face spray/tape; clarify whether equipment, setup or dynamics contribute |
| Distance from ball varies between attempts | Does spacing actually vary measurably? Does delivery change after address? |
| Wedges/shorter irons generally feel more reliable; longer clubs have had right-curving misses | Compare launch, strike and dispersion **by club** rather than assuming a single cause |
| Player feels excessive movement sometimes disrupts impact | Determine whether motion is appropriate athletic rotation or loss of posture/low-point control |
| More partial wedge/chipping practice is desired | Assess basic carry/landing/rollout controls before prescribing |
| Hybrid, fairway wood and new fitted driver need calibrated practice | Confirm fitted club specifications and establish stock shots |

None of these is a proven biomechanical diagnosis. Past TrackMan values and simulator clips are cataloged in the knowledge history, but raw shot provenance is not yet fully confirmed.

## 3. Suggested first lesson — swing analysis, not a technique overhaul

**Before correcting anything:** Agree on a reasonable shot sample and capture comparable good/missed swings. For each chosen club, keep target, lie and swing intention stable; record strike region, turf contact, start line/curve, and measured instrument values when available. Face-on and down-the-line video are useful, subject to capture quality and consent.

**Coach decision:** Which **one** issue is most likely holding consistency back? What evidence supports it, what alternatives remain plausible, and what is the first controlled retest?

**Exit:** One confirmed observation, one coach-approved priority and drill, a realistic practice frequency, and the exact data to bring next time.

Detailed worksheet: [Swing assessment template](SWING_ASSESSMENT_TEMPLATE.md).

## 4. Four-lesson structure

| Session | Subject | What to bring back |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Swing Analysis | Address, swing and impact baseline; compare good/miss | Confirmed top priority, coach-approved cue, before/after sample |
| 2 — Approaches | Partial wedges, chips, landing windows and distance control | One or two dependable shots and measurable task |
| 3 — Irons | Stock iron pattern, contact, direction and gapping | Comparable carry/dispersion, established setup |
| 4 — Long Clubs | Driver/wood/hybrid from appropriate lies and tees | Playable tee-shot option, stock ball flight and equipment fit notes |

Further detail: [Coaching brief](COACHING_BRIEF.md).
