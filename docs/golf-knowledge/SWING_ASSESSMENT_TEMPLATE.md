## B. Compare mechanics and contact

- Setup: grip, posture, distance, alignment, ball location.
- Takeaway and backswing: depth, balance, rotation; do not prescribe an arbitrary 'correct' wrist hinge.
- Transition: pressure and sequence; compare good vs poor shots.
- Downswing/impact: low point, strike region, dynamic face/path if measured.
- Post-impact: extension, balance, finish; avoid asking the golfer to freeze at impact.
- Camera limitation: face-on and down-the-line are complementary; perspective distortion and frame rate matter.

## D. Questions to ask Justin

1. Are the alleged heel strikes demonstrated with impact tape/spray or another measurable method?
2. Does setup distance explain misses, or does the delivery move outward during the downswing?
3. For chunked shots, is the low point changing, or is there another identifiable contact problem?
4. Are face and path measurements available, and does the observed ball flight agree?
5. Which **single correction** should be rehearsed for the next 2 weeks?
6. What is the objective pass/retest criterion before progressing to long clubs?

## E. Exit artifacts

A tagged baseline swing pair, instructor-attributed primary fault/uncertainty, one approved drill, a shot-log baseline, the next reassessment date, and notes on equipment fit vs swing mechanics.
