# Justin Coaching Brief — Four Remaining Lessons

**Planning status:** Proposed by the player. Justin retains authority over technical instruction and session sequence.

## Goal

Build a repeatable, self-correcting swing and eventually play a beginner-friendly amateur event. Priority is **repeatability and playable dispersion**, not one exceptional maximum-distance shot.

## Revised lesson sequence (owner preference)

| Lesson | Focus | Deliverable | Instructor decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Comprehensive swing analysis** | Baseline video + strike/flight/low-point diagnostics; prioritized issue; one validated drill | Identify what explains flush vs chunk and reported heel bias |
| 2 | **Approach game** | Partial-wedge / approach distance control, landing targets; include chipping needs | Decide whether chipping merits separate follow-up |
| 3 | **Irons** | Stock and controlled iron shot, address repeatability, start/curve, carry and dispersion | Which irons to standardize first |
| 4 | **Driver / woods / hybrid** | Tee-shot and turf-shot options, launch and dispersion, fitted-driver calibration | Establish reliable go-to tee club and long-game practice |

## Current questions (NOT diagnoses)

- Why were some recent shots flushed and others chunked under apparently similar setup?
- How much strike bias is truly toward the heel? Is it caused by setup, dynamic movement or equipment?
- Does pre-shot spacing drift between repetitions?
- What is the playable, repeatable baseline for the hybrid, 3-wood and fitted driver?
- Does a compact rehearsal improve readiness without creating unnecessary swing thoughts?

## Between-lesson protocol

Log a 5–10 shot baseline per chosen club. Retest **one** instructor-approved cue, same club/lie/target if possible. Save counts and misses, not just best shots. Return to Justin with the comparison and one question. Avoid introducing simultaneous untested wrist, knee, posture and path fixes.

## Competition readiness (future, separate coaching stream)

Establish real-course scoring baseline, pace/rules knowledge, putting, recovery and course management. Verify handicap and local event eligibility before setting a date. No promise that four lessons alone constitute competition readiness.
