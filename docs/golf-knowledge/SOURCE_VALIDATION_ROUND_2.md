# Source and Technical Validation — Round 2 (2026-11-26)

**Status:** Source triangulation in progress; not instructor certification or a complete research systematic review. This file records which broad facts have independent primary-source support and which personal causal claims are still unverified.

## Primary references reviewed

1. **TrackMan Help Center — [Club Path](https://support.trackmangolf.com/hc/en-us/articles/39724275421211-Parameters-Club-Path-Tee-to-Green).** Negative denotes path left of target line; positive denotes right. Terminology/reference supported.
2. **TrackMan Help Center — [Face Angle](https://support.trackmangolf.com/hc/en-us/articles/39724466444059-Parameters-Face-Angle-Tee-to-Green).** Face-angle orientation at impact relative to target; major influence on start direction. Definition supported.
3. **TrackMan Help Center — [Face-to-Path](https://support.trackmangolf.com/hc/en-us/articles/39724525751707-Parameters-Face-to-Path-Tee-to-Green).** Difference between face angle and club path; ball curve guidance assumes centered strike. Definition supported; personal diagnosis not established.
4. **TrackMan Help Center — [Swing Direction](https://support.trackmangolf.com/hc/en-us/articles/39724812068379-Parameters-Swing-Direction-Tee-to-Green).** Distinct from club path; important correction for conceptual swing-plane model.
5. **TrackMan — [Club Data Definitions](https://www.trackman.com/blog/club-data-definitions).** Club delivery terminology and relationship to ball flight; does not validate historical player figures.
6. **PGA of America — [Golf drills and low-point awareness](https://www.pga.com/story/pga-coach-shares-4-easy-golf-drills-to-improve-your-swing).** Teaching drills aimed at ball-first contact and low-point awareness. Supports candidate *instruction topic*, not one-size-fits-all treatment or diagnosis.
7. **USGA — [Clubs](https://www.usga.org/content/usga/home-page/rules-hub/topics/clubs.html).** Multiple conforming drivers are allowed within the 14-club total; equipment *value* remains unverified by rule alone.

## Still NOT validated

- **Personal root cause:** no proof that reported heel strikes resulted from spacing, an outward hand move, equipment, wrist release or any single cause.
- **Chunked shots:** no reliable frame-by-frame/turf-first matching for each missed shot; hypothesis about low point ≠ confirmed cause.
- **Player historical TrackMan trend:** values in conversation excerpt are not original dated shot records; no verified club/session matching.
- **Hula-hoop angles:** an illustrative club-dependent tilted hoop is not a measurement of an individual golfer's 3D swing plane.
- **Candidate drill efficacy:** public instructional references do not justify a personalized drill prescription, fixed success threshold or mastery award for this user.
- **Fitting result:** no original order/spec sheet demonstrating which new driver or shaft performs better or why two drivers would be preferable.
- **Amateur readiness:** no verified scoring or handicap baseline.
- **Biomechanical claims about knee/wrist:** not established by simulator replay footage alone.
